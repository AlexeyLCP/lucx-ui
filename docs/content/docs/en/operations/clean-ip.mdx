In filtered networks some Cloudflare edge IPs respond far better than others.
**Clean IP** (`/cleanip`) probes random addresses from the published
Cloudflare IPv4 ranges on the port you choose and ranks the healthy ones —
TLS-verified addresses first (handshake against your SNI), then by latency.

## How the scan works

- The panel samples `Sample` random addresses (default 256), probes them
  concurrently (32 workers, `Timeout` per probe), and keeps at most `Top
  results` (default 30).
- With **Verify TLS handshake** on, an address is dropped when the handshake
  against `SNI` (default `www.cloudflare.com`) fails — such addresses are
  useless as edges.
- The scan is synchronous: the response arrives when the batch is done
  (worst case ~10 s at the defaults).

## Use the result

The scan is **read-only** — the panel never changes client configs or
outbound settings by itself. Copy a healthy address and use it as the
client-side `address` for your VLESS share link (keep your domain in
SNI/Host), or paste it into the outbound target of your choice. See
[VLESS through CDN](/docs/operations/vless-cdn) for the full recipe.

Scanning from the panel host only shows edges as seen *from that host* —
healthy-for-the-server does not always mean healthy-for-the-client, so test
the winner from the client side too.
